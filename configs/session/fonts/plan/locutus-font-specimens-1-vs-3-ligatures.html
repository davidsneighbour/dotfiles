If the ON and OFF rows look identical, that browser/rendering path is not applying the requested OpenType substitutions. The fonts being online is not the problem: webfonts can expose ligatures normally. Monaspace distributes its ligatures across OpenType features/stylistic sets, while Recursive exposes code ligatures through dlig in the standard family and also ships code-oriented builds with them enabled more aggressively.
